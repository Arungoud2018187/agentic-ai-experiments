Agentic AI

Agentic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that can autonomously perform tasks by reasoning about goals, making decisions, using tools, and adapting their actions based on results.

An AI agent typically receives a goal, determines what actions are required, executes those actions, observes the results, and continues until the task is completed.

Agentic systems can use tools such as databases, APIs, search engines, calculators, and file systems.
